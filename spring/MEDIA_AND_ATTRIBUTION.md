All 14 film stills in this article are actual frames extracted from the supplied source video and saved as descriptive JPEG filenames with corresponding `-640.jpg` derivatives. The stills were re-encoded for website presentation without changing the composition.
Original analytical diagrams are HTML/CSS visual models, not stills, and are intentionally excluded from the still-image gallery and archive preview.
Figures and their source times are recorded in `media-figure-map.json` and are ordered to match the article and PDF.
