# Sintel — visual asset provenance

The 15 local film stills are exact compositions selected from the user-provided file `sintel-2048-surround.mp4` (2048×872, 14:48), and re-encoded as JPEG without intentional crops, upscaling or synthetic changes. Smaller -640 variants are responsive representations of the same frames. Original visual diagrams remain HTML/CSS figures and are not part of the photo viewer or homepage previews.

**Source**: *Sintel* (2010), directed by Colin Levy, Blender Foundation / Durian Open Movie Project.
