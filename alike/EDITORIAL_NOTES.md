# Alike — image, research and deployment notes

**Publication:** Filmmic Foundations (2026-10-21)
**Film:** *Alike* (2015), directed by Daniel Martínez Lara and Rafa Cano Méndez
**Official film:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kQjtK32mGJQ

This is an original critical interpretation. Its original cover illustration and three analytical schematics are **not** film frames. The analysis webpage includes credited, externally hosted publicity imagery; those images remain owned by the film's rights holders and require their hosts to remain available. The locally packaged PDF contains the interpretive cover illustration and diagrams, not those external film stills.

Please watch the short and verify specific visual claims against the film before publication. Credits and major factual claims have been cross-checked against the official film channel, production notes, the Goya Awards, OECD and Ryan & Deci.

The PDF uses publication-level attribution and removes individual creator metadata. It does not make claims of exclusive image ownership over the film. The new article reuses the site's existing navigation, citation, settings, PDF controls, related-reading interface and viewer logic.
